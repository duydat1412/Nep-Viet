## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dialogue between Vietnamese dynastic heritage and contemporary high-fashion editorial digital culture. The aesthetic bridges the tactile warmth of handcrafted artisan craft (Giấy Dó handmade mulberry paper, sơn mài lacquerware, Hue imperial textiles) with the razor-sharp clarity of high-end mobile digital fashion lookbooks. 

Targeted at a culture-conscious generation seeking contemporary ways to interpret and wear traditional silhouettes (such as Áo Dài, Áo Tấc, and Nhật Bình), the visual narrative departs from sterile e-commerce layouts in favor of an opulent, magazine-style experience. The emotional atmosphere is poised, warm, tactile, and ceremonial, balancing the reverence of historical textile arts with kinetic, forward-looking styling tools. 

Key visual principles:
- **Artisan Paper Substrates:** Surfaces recall fibrous, warm, organic mulberry paper rather than synthetic sterile whites.
- **Imperial Contrast:** High-contrast lacquer-ink typography anchored with subtle gold foils and vermilion seal stamps.
- **Lookbook Asymmetry:** Editorial layouts featuring expansive verticality, generous breathing space, deliberate typography pairings, and micro-ornamental details derived from imperial motifs.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in Vietnamese imperial craft, court costumery, and regional dyeing traditions:

- **Primary (`#B5362B` Vermilion Lacquer Seal):** Used for focal action points, verification badges, celebratory accents, and seal-style highlights reminiscent of historical vermilion signature chops (*triện*).
- **Secondary (`#C8963E` Imperial Hue Gold):** Represents dynastic embroidery, gilded borders, active selection strokes, and fine decorative hairline dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#26466D` Hoi An Indigo):** Used for depth accents, secondary categorization, contextual tags, and cool contrast against warm paper grounds.
- **Neutral (`#2B2118` Deep Lacquer Ink):** The fundamental ink color for headlines, icons, and primary text, replacing cold generic blacks with an organic, carbon-rich lacquer tone.

### Palette System & Canvas Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#F6EFE0` Giấy Dó Paper):** The primary warm background providing a tactile, artisanal canvas.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#FDFBF7` Bleached Mulberry Paper):** Used for cards, sheets, and active panels requiring lift.
- **Subdued Canvas (`#EDE4D0` Aged Paper):** Reserved for recessed panels, wells, segmented controls, and inactive states.
- **Text Muted (`#4A3B2C` Roasted Ochre):** Body secondary text, supporting captions, and metadata.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E0D4BE`):** Hairline separation preserving paper-grain authenticity without harsh contrast.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines imperial literary elegance with modern interface ergonomics:

- **Display & Headline Roles (`Playfair Display`):** Delivers dramatic editorial scale, high stroke contrast, and classic cadence for editorial titles, curation names, lookbook covers, and heritage stories. It mirrors the cadence of high-end fashion publications like Vogue while retaining the prestige of court documents.
- **Body & Functional Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Provides geometric clarity, wide counters, and exceptional legibility at small sizes across mobile viewports. Handles AI styling recommendations, fabric composition breakdowns, metadata tags, and touch targets.
- **Styling Rules:**
  - Display numbers and imperial collection names use italicized or title-cased `Playfair Display`.
  - Micro-labels, metadata tags, and currency representations use uppercase `Plus Jakarta Sans` with tracked letter-spacing (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to echo editorial credits.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout embraces an editorial lookbook model, moving away from dense utility grids in favor of deliberate pacing, rhythmic asymmetrical content blocks, and intentional negative space.

### Grid Framework
- **Mobile (Base):** 4-column fluid layout, `margin` of `1.25rem`, `gutter` of `1rem`. Optimized for full-bleed fashion photography with inset floating controls.
- **Tablet (600px - 1024px):** 8-column layout, `margin` of `2rem`, `gutter` of `1.25rem`. Facilitates split-screen AI curation (look on the left, garment anatomy and styling notes on the right).
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout, centered max-width container of `1280px`, `margin-desktop` of `2.5rem`, `gutter-desktop` of `1.5rem`.

### Spacing Rhythm
- **Micro Space (`space-xs` to `space-sm`):** Binds garment attribute tags, swatch indicators, and seal badges.
- **Component Flow (`space-md` to `space-lg`):** Governs card interior padding, input field heights, and form rows.
- **Editorial Breathing Room (`space-xl`):** Establishes pauses between editorial features, lookbook shifts, and generational trend capsules.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is conveyed through tactile layers of parchment and lacquered materials rather than heavy synthetic drop shadows.

- **Layer 0 (Canvas Base):** Textured Giấy Dó paper tone (`#F6EFE0`).
- **Layer 1 (Card & Substrate Elevation):** Surface `#FDFBF7` bounded by ultra-fine hairline borders (`#E0D4BE`, 1px solid). Shadow is an ambient, paper-settled diffusion: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Layer 2 (Floating Sheets & Lookbook Drawers):** High-opacity frosted mulberry veil (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.88)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`). Shadows are tinted warm ink: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 (Modal Dialogs & Imperial Seals):** `#FDFBF7` surrounded by a dual outline: an inner 1px border of `#C8963E` (Imperial Gold at 30% opacity) and an ambient shadow of `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(43, 33, 24, 0.14)`.

## Shapes

The shape vocabulary balances structured architectural proportions with organic softness:

- **Containers & Fashion Cards:** Medium roundedness (`0.5rem` / `8px`) preserves crisp editorial lines reminiscent of physical prints, fashion plates, and lacquer panels.
- **Floating Modals & Curated Sheets:** Larger roundedness (`rounded-lg` / `1rem` and `rounded-xl` / `1.5rem`) adds human warmth and touchable ergonomics on mobile viewports.
- **Interactive Micro-Elements (Chips, Pills, Action Seals):** Full pill curvature to counterweight the structured editorial serif headers and provide immediate tactile affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Imperial Lacquer):** Solid vermilion fill (`#B5362B`), lacquer-ink text inverted to off-white (`#FDFBF7`), `label-lg` styling, pill-rounded, with micro gold-foil hairline active ring (`#C8963E`).
- **Secondary Action (Gold Leaf Outline):** Transparent paper ground, 1px solid border in Imperial Gold (`#C8963E`), typography in `#2B2118`. Hover/active shifts background to `#EDE4D0`.
- **Tertiary Action (Ink Minimal):** Borderless, `#2B2118` text with an animated bottom border (0.5px ink stroke).

### Chips & Badges
- **Cultural Material Chips (e.g., Lụa Vạn Phúc, Gấm, Sơn Mài):** Pill-shaped, background `#EDE4D0`, border `1px solid #E0D4BE`, text `#4A3B2C` in `label-md`.
- **Status/AI Match Badges:** High-gloss pill with Hoi An indigo tint (`rgba(38, 70, 109, 0.08)`), text `#26466D`, and an optional gold leaf dot icon (`#C8963E`).

### Cards & Lookbook Tiles
- **Garment & Outfit Card:** Surface `#FDFBF7`, 1px border `#E0D4BE`, `0.5rem` border radius. Images maintain an editorial 3:4 portrait aspect ratio. Card footers feature asymmetric typographic locks with display serif titles and monospace or tracked metadata badges.
- **AI Recommendation Overlay:** Floating frosted glass card (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.92)` backdrop blur) nested over garment imagery, outlined with an imperial gold micro-hairline.

### Form Inputs & Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Crisp recessed background (`#EDE4D0` at 50% opacity), bottom border active treatment (`1.5px solid #C8963E`), text `#2B2118`, placeholder in `#4A3B2C` (50% opacity). Zero harsh focus rings; focus is marked by warm gold glow and label elevation.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Handcrafted aesthetic; selected state fills with `#B5362B` holding an off-white emblem checkmark; radios use a concentric ring of `#C8963E` encasing an ink center.

### Specialized Heritage Components
- **Imperial Seal Stamp (*Dấu Triện*):** Square or rounded-square insignia with vermilion background (`#B5362B`), embossed border, and stylized monogram marking verified heritage provenance.
- **Lookbook Splitter:** Slender horizontal hairline (`#E0D4BE`) centered by a small four-point gold diamond motif (`#C8963E`).
